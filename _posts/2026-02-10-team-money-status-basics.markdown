Team Money Status gives you a simple check of **Expected**, **Current**, and **Difference**.

Use it as your weekly confidence check:

1. Confirm expected parent payments are up to date.
2. Confirm current team money reflects your latest transactions.
3. Investigate any non-zero difference before the next update to parents.
